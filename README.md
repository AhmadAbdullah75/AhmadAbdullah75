#### 🩺 [Lung Cancer ML — Replication & Improvisation](https://github.com/AhmadAbdullah75/lung-cancer-replication-improvisation)
*Found a critical flaw in a published paper — and fixed it.*

Replicated a 2025 BioMed Research International paper and discovered 84.8% duplicate rows were inflating the reported 99% accuracy. Corrected the honest accuracy to 95.65%, then expanded the voting ensemble from 3 to 6 models for a +0.62% cross-validated F1 gain and 38.3% faster inference. Now co-authoring the improved paper with a faculty supervisor.

`Python` `scikit-learn` `Ensemble Models` `Research`
**[GitHub](https://github.com/AhmadAbdullah75/lung-cancer-replication-improvisation)**
